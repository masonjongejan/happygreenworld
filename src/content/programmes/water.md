## About This Programme

Water is life — yet billions of people around the world lack access to clean, safe water, and that number is growing. Children who grow up understanding the importance of water conservation become adults who protect this precious resource.

Happy Green World's Water & Conservation programme connects children to the water cycle, the threats facing freshwater systems, and the simple habits that can make a real difference — in their homes, their schools, and their communities.

## What Children Learn

- Where water comes from and how the water cycle works
- The difference between freshwater and saltwater, and why freshwater is scarce
- How human activity affects rivers, lakes, and oceans
- Practical water-saving habits for home and school
- The connection between waste, pollution, and water quality
- Why clean water access is a global challenge — and how to think about solutions

## Localisation

Water challenges look different in every country we work in. A community in Senegal faces very different water realities than one in the Netherlands or Thailand. Our materials are adapted to reflect these local contexts — using illustrations, examples, and activities that resonate with children's lived experience.
