Marlou is an environmental consultant based in Perth, Australia. Her passion for environmental education began in Cairo in 2009, where she witnessed the devastating effects of unmanaged waste firsthand. She founded Happy Green World in 2013 with the conviction that lasting environmental change starts with children. Today she leads the foundation's global partnerships and continues to develop new programmes alongside local communities.
