Petra is a Netherlands-based graphic designer who has co-created Happy Green World's visual identity from the very beginning. She designs all of the foundation's educational materials — creating bright, warm illustrations that are carefully adapted to reflect the cultural context of each country we work in. Her artwork makes complex environmental ideas accessible and joyful for children aged 6–16.
