Energy & Climate

Empowering children to understand energy, climate change, and the choices that shape our future.

About This Programme

Energy touches every part of modern life — and our choices about how we generate and use it are shaping the planet our children will inherit. Happy Green World’s Energy & Climate programme demystifies these concepts for young learners, connecting everyday actions to global consequences in ways that are age-appropriate, empowering, and actionable.

What Children Learn

  • Where energy comes from: fossil fuels, solar, wind, and other renewable sources
  • What climate change is and how it connects to energy use
  • How to identify energy waste at home and at school
  • Simple, practical habits for reducing energy consumption
  • The relationship between energy, transport, food, and the environment
  • Why climate change affects different communities around the world in different ways

Localisation

Energy education is adapted for each country’s context. In some communities, the focus is on solar potential; in others, it’s on the environmental cost of burning waste for fuel. Our materials meet children where they are, with illustrations and examples drawn from their own environment.
